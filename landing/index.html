What arrives

The same estimate, written twice for the two people who have to agree on it. Neither is a summary of the other, and the totals cannot drift apart — they are the same figures, rendered twice.

Three decisions

The parts that are not negotiable.

Every quotation tool can produce a table. These are the choices that decide whether you can put your name on what comes out of it.

The server owns the arithmetic

Every total is recomputed after the model answers, so a document can never show a figure that disagrees with the sum of its own rows.

No currency conversion, anywhere

A job for Berlin is priced as a German job in euros — not a local quote run through an exchange rate. There is no FX table in the system.

Nothing is corrected quietly

Hit a budget and the effort moves, never the rates. Anything stretched, capped or dropped is printed on the page that changed.
